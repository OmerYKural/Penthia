Coverage you can count on

3-year limited hardware warranty

Penthia Solutions provides a three-year limited hardware warranty on eligible interactive display panels purchased directly through Penthia Solutions.

Warranty term

Coverage begins on the confirmed delivery date. If installation is provided directly or coordinated by Penthia Solutions, the warranty begins on the installation completion date instead.

What's covered

Defects in materials or workmanship and unexpected hardware failure under normal classroom use.

  • Display panel and touch system
  • Internal electronics and Android board
  • Speakers and power supply
  • Built-in camera/microphone modules, if included
  • OPS Windows computer, if purchased through Penthia

Shipping & delivery issues

Products arriving damaged, defective, or non-functional must be reported promptly with photos, videos, packaging images, and delivery documentation. Claims may be covered if reported within the required window and properly documented.

What's not covered

Accidental damage, misuse, abuse, vandalism, impact damage, cracked glass from external force, liquid or fire damage, power surge damage, improper installation, unauthorized repair or modification, cosmetic wear, lost accessories, consumable items, network configuration issues, software account issues, or damage caused by students, staff, contractors, installers, movers, or other third parties.

Examples not covered

A student throwing an object at the board, damage during school-handled installation, dropping the board, moving it incorrectly, striking the glass, or damage from improper mounting.

Warranty remedy

For a covered issue, Penthia may provide troubleshooting, replacement parts, repair coordination, or product replacement at its discretion. Photos, videos, serial numbers, remote diagnosis, and manufacturer technical review may be required before approval.

This limited warranty applies only to products purchased directly through Penthia Solutions and is subject to the final written warranty terms provided with your quote or invoice.

Common questions

Questions about Penthia boards

Are Penthia smartboards just televisions with touch overlays?

+

No. Penthia Vertex boards are purpose-built interactive classroom smartboards. Depending on the model and configuration, they include Android-based operation, 4K touch, 7H anti-glare glass, annotation tools, whiteboard features, screen sharing, Google app support on Vertex Pro and Elite, optional Windows OPS, and optional camera/microphone. They are not consumer TVs.

Do Penthia Pro and Elite models support Google apps?

+

Yes. Google apps are supported on Penthia Vertex Pro and Vertex Elite on supported configurations. Vertex Pro runs Android 14, Vertex Elite runs Android 15. Schools can use Google Classroom, Drive, Docs, Slides, Chrome, Gmail, and more. Confirm app availability for your specific configuration at deployment.

Are Penthia boards Google EDLA certified?

+

The hardware platform is manufactured by a company that produces EDLA-certified models and is capable of EDLA certification. However, EDLA certification is not currently issued under the Penthia brand name. In practice, Google Workspace services — Chrome, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet, Classroom — work through Google accounts on supported configurations. For schools that require official EDLA certification as a procurement requirement, Penthia is evaluating the certification process for future Penthia-branded models.

Do Penthia boards require Windows OPS to be useful?

+

No. Windows OPS is optional for schools that need full Windows desktop functionality. Vertex Pro and Elite include Android-based operation, Google app support, annotation, whiteboard, screen sharing, and 4K touch without any Windows module. Windows OPS adds desktop software like Windows 11, Office, or other Windows-only apps.

What Android versions do the Vertex boards use?

+

Vertex Elite runs Android 15 on the RK3588 platform. Vertex Pro runs Android 14 on the 311D2 (A311D2) platform. Vertex Standard runs Android 14 on the T985 platform. QS3 has no built-in Android — it's designed for use with external Windows, Mac, Linux, or OPS devices.

Do Penthia boards include camera or microphone options?

+

Yes. Vertex Pro and Elite support an optional 48MP AI camera and 8-microphone array configuration. The AI camera adds speaker tracking, voice tracking, and gesture controls. This supports Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams for hybrid learning and video conferencing. Confirm camera configuration at the time of purchase.

What does the Penthia warranty cover?

+

Penthia Solutions provides a 3-year limited hardware warranty covering defects in materials, workmanship, and unexpected hardware failure under normal classroom use — including the display panel, touch system, internal electronics, Android board, speakers, power supply, and optional camera/microphone or OPS Windows computer if purchased through Penthia. It does not cover accidental damage, misuse, liquid or fire damage, improper installation, or damage caused by students, staff, or third parties. See full warranty terms for details.
