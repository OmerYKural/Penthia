Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Are Penthia smartboards just televisions with touch overlays?

+

No. Penthia Vertex boards are purpose-built interactive classroom smartboards. Depending on the model and configuration, they include Android-based operation, 4K touch, anti-glare glass, annotation tools, whiteboard features, screen sharing, Google app support on Vertex Pro and Elite, optional Windows OPS, and camera or microphone options. They are not consumer TVs.

Do Penthia Pro and Elite models support Google apps?

+

Yes. Google apps are supported on Vertex Pro (Android 14) and Vertex Elite (Android 15) on supported configurations — including Google Classroom, Drive, Docs, Slides, Chrome, and Gmail. App availability should be confirmed for each school's specific requirements.

Do Penthia boards require Windows OPS to be useful?

+

No. Windows OPS is fully optional. Vertex Pro and Vertex Elite include Android-based operation, Google app support, annotation tools, whiteboard features, screen sharing, and 4K touch without any Windows module. OPS is available for schools that need Windows-only software.

Do Penthia boards include camera or microphone options?

+

Yes. Vertex Pro and Vertex Elite support optional 48MP AI camera and 8-microphone array configurations. The AI camera adds speaker tracking, voice tracking, and gesture controls. These support Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, and recorded presentations. Confirm configurations at the time of purchase.

What Android versions do Vertex Pro and Vertex Elite use?

+

Vertex Pro uses Android 14 (311D2 platform). Vertex Elite uses Android 15 (RK3588 platform). Vertex Standard also runs Android 14 (T985 platform). QS3 Series has no built-in Android and works with external Windows, Mac, Linux, or OPS devices.

Are Penthia boards Google EDLA certified?

+

The hardware platform is capable of EDLA certification and is manufactured by a company that produces EDLA-certified models. However, EDLA certification is not currently issued under the Penthia brand name. In practice, customers still have full access to Google Workspace — Chrome, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet, and Classroom — through Google accounts and connected devices. For schools requiring official EDLA certification as a procurement requirement, Penthia is currently evaluating the certification process for future Penthia-branded models.
