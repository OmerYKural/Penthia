Common Questions

Common Questions About Penthia Boards

Are Penthia smartboards just televisions with touch overlays?

+

No. Penthia Vertex boards are interactive classroom smartboards, not standalone televisions with touch overlays. Depending on the selected model and configuration, Penthia boards include Android-based operation, 4K touch, anti-glare glass, annotation tools, whiteboard features, screen sharing, Google app support on Vertex Pro and Vertex Elite models, optional Windows OPS modules, and camera or microphone options. They are purpose-built classroom displays, not consumer TVs.

Do Penthia Pro and Elite models support Google apps?

+

Yes. Google apps are supported on Penthia Vertex Pro and Penthia Vertex Elite models. Vertex Pro models run Android 14, and Vertex Elite models run Android 15. Schools can use supported Google applications on Pro and Elite models — including Google Classroom, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Slides, Chrome, and Gmail — depending on the final configuration and deployment setup. App availability should be confirmed for each school's specific requirements.

Do Penthia boards require Windows OPS to be useful?

+

No. Windows OPS is an optional addition for schools that need full Windows desktop functionality, but it is not required for everyday classroom use. Penthia Vertex Pro and Vertex Elite models include Android-based operation, Google app support, annotation tools, whiteboard features, screen sharing, and 4K touch functionality without any Windows module. Windows OPS is available for schools that want additional desktop software capability such as Windows 11, Microsoft Office, or other Windows-only software.

Do Penthia boards include camera or microphone options?

+

Yes. Penthia boards can be configured with camera and microphone options depending on the selected model and purchase configuration. Vertex Pro and Vertex Elite models support optional 48MP AI camera and microphone configurations. These options can support classroom communication, hybrid learning, video conferencing in tools like Zoom or Google Meet, and recorded presentations. Camera and microphone configurations should be confirmed at the time of purchase.

What Android versions do Vertex Pro and Vertex Elite use?

+

Penthia Vertex Pro boards use Android 14, based on the 311D2 platform. Penthia Vertex Elite boards use Android 15, based on the RK3588 platform. The Vertex Standard also runs Android 14, based on the T985 platform. Both Vertex Pro and Vertex Elite support Google apps on supported configurations. The QS3 Series does not have a built-in Android system and is designed to work with external Windows, Mac, Linux, or OPS devices.
